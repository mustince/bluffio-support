Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play Bluffio?

Add and select players, choose categories, then pass the phone so everyone can privately reveal their role. Citizens know the secret word, while the Imposter tries to blend in. Discuss, vote, and reveal the result.

Does Bluffio require an account?

No. Bluffio does not require registration, a login, or an account.

Can I play offline?

The local pass-and-play game can be played offline. Advertising and some platform services may require an internet connection.

Which languages are supported?

Bluffio supports English and Turkish.

How do I report a bug?

Email bluffioapp@gmail.com. If possible, include your device model, operating-system version, and a short description of what happened.
