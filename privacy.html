Bluffio: Imposter Party Game

Bluffio Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026

Overview

Bluffio is a local pass-and-play party game. Bluffio does not require you to register, create an account, or log in to play.

Information stored on your device

Gameplay takes place primarily on your device. Bluffio may store local information such as app preferences, selected language, saved player names, game statistics, and related gameplay history. This information supports app features and remains stored locally unless you remove it through available app or device controls.

Advertising and Google AdMob

Bluffio uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Depending on your platform settings, consent choices, region, and Google's policies, Google Mobile Ads may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, app interactions, diagnostics, and ad-related data.

Google's processing is governed by its own terms and privacy policies. You can learn more from Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

How information is used

Local app information is used to provide Bluffio's gameplay, preferences, player library, and statistics features. Advertising data may be used by Google to deliver, measure, secure, and improve ads in accordance with Google's policies and your applicable settings or consent choices.

Sale of personal information

Bluffio does not sell personal information. Third-party advertising processing is performed by Google under Google's own privacy terms and policies.

Your choices

Your device and platform may provide privacy and advertising controls, including controls for advertising identifiers, tracking permissions, personalized advertising, app data, and stored application data. Available controls vary by platform, operating-system version, and region.

Children's privacy

Bluffio is not specifically directed to children. The app does not require account registration or ask players to submit personal information directly to Bluffio. Advertising services may process data as described above and apply their own age-related requirements and protections. Parents and guardians should review device privacy controls and Google's policies when allowing a child to use the app.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Bluffio's features, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact bluffioapp@gmail.com.
